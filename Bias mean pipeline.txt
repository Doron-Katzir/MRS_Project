1. Start with 36 temporal MRS sets.

2. Choose a division size.
   Example: 6 scans per group.

3. Average the data into groups.
   Division_6 gives 6 averaged spectra.

4. Fit each averaged spectrum using LCModel.

5. Extract metabolite concentrations from LCModel.

6. For each metabolite, organize concentrations by division.

7. For each division, compute:
      mean concentration
      variance
      number of values

8. Choose a reference division, usually Division_36.

9. Compute bias:
      alpha_i = mean(Division_i) - mean(Division_36)

10. Convert bias to percent:
      percentBias = 100 * alpha_i / mean(Division_36)

11. Use Welch ANOVA as an unequal-variance test of whether division means differ.
